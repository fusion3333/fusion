# STAY Observations V1

## O-STAY-001 — STAY must be modeled as night allocation
A Korea trip of N days does not imply N nights in one region. The engine needs total duration plus a sequence of region-night allocations.

## O-STAY-002 — Official survey and telecom sensors observe different objects
Survey answers describe traveler-reported trip/stay/accommodation behavior. Telecom observes presence and overnight proxies. Agreement strengthens evidence; disagreement is itself a diagnostic signal.

## O-STAY-003 — Accommodation choice is multi-modal
Hotel dominance in survey reporting does not justify collapsing all lodging into hotels. Friends/relatives, guesthouse/hostel, motel/inn, minbak/pension and other categories must remain separate.

## O-STAY-004 — Supply capacity and consumed nights are different
MCST registered establishments/rooms describe formal capacity context. They do not reveal foreign occupancy, actual availability, OTA visibility or sellability.

## O-STAY-005 — STAY is a bridge between MOVE and SPEND
A regional overnight creates a time window for local activities and spending. Day visitation without overnight and overnight visitation must therefore be separate states.

## O-STAY-006 — Regional Night Leakage is an opportunity signal
High visit/search interest + low overnight conversion can indicate a day-trip trap, weak accommodation fit, last-mile friction, weak evening product, pricing mismatch or booking friction. It is not automatically an accommodation shortage.

## O-STAY-007 — OTA data are independent commercial sensors
Booking.com/Agoda search and booking publications can reveal platform-specific demand momentum, but cannot be treated as total inbound lodging demand without market-share denominators.

## Candidate diagnostic objects (not final KTOF equations)
- OvernightConversionGap: high regional presence with low overnight persistence.
- NightAllocationConcentration: concentration of a traveler's nights in gateway cities versus secondary regions.
- StayExtensionOpportunity: conditions associated with an additional night after controlling for segment/season/access.
- AccommodationAccessibilityGap: desired lodging attributes versus foreigner-usable/bookable supply.
- StayValueLeakage: activity demand exists in a region but nights/spend remain captured by another region.

## Causal guardrail
Observed association between accommodation supply and longer stay does not establish that adding rooms causes longer stay. WHY, price, access, event calendar, party type and itinerary structure can jointly determine both.
