# REAL SELLABLE SUPPLY — Observations V1

## O1 Supply is a funnel, not a count
RegisteredSupply != OperatingSupply != DiscoverableSupply != BookableSupply != PayableSupply != ConsumableSupply.

## O2 Sellability is segment/channel/time dependent
The same product can be sellable for a Korean resident, unusable for a short-term foreign visitor, available on an OTA but unavailable direct, or change after payment/identity policy updates.

## O3 Foreign-language information alone is insufficient
Multilingual content without visible price, live inventory, usable identity path, foreign payment, confirmation and cancellation/refund is not transaction-ready supply.

## O4 Workarounds are hidden transaction costs
Emailing staff, asking a Korean friend, paying through a more expensive intermediary or manually transferring money can convert a failed native path into a completed purchase. Record this as SUCCESS_WITH_FRICTION, not clean sellability.

## O5 Capacity is part of supply
A globally discoverable experience with only a few foreigner-usable slots is not equivalent to a scalable supplier. Need operating days, slots/day, party limits, blackout dates, lead time, seasonality and willingness to expand.

## O6 Supplier willingness is a latent edge
A culturally valuable asset may technically exist yet have zero willingness to accept foreign customers or package with partners. Conversely a supplier with weak digital distribution but strong willingness can be a high-value commercialization candidate.

## O7 Real Sellable Supply enables a new loss decomposition
For N discovered assets:
Loss_operating = 1 - operating/existing
Loss_discovery = 1 - discoverable/operating
Loss_language = 1 - understandable/discoverable
Loss_booking = 1 - bookable/understandable
Loss_payment = 1 - payable/bookable
Loss_capacity = 1 - capacity_usable/payable
Loss_completion = 1 - completed/attempted
Do not compute nationally until denominators are observed.

## Candidate opportunity signals (diagnostic only)
- HIGH_DEMAND + HIGH_ASSET_QUALITY + LOW_SELLABILITY = Commercialization Island
- HIGH_DISCOVERY + LOW_BOOKABILITY = Booking Bridge Gap
- HIGH_BOOKABILITY + LOW_FOREIGN_PAYMENT = Payment Accessibility Gap
- HIGH_SELLABILITY + LOW_CAPACITY = Capacity Bottleneck
- LOW_DISCOVERY + HIGH_GROUND_QUALITY + HIGH_WILLINGNESS = Hidden Gem Distribution Opportunity
- HIGH_PAIN + HIGH_WORKAROUND_SUCCESS = Intermediation Opportunity

No KTOF weights are assigned here.
