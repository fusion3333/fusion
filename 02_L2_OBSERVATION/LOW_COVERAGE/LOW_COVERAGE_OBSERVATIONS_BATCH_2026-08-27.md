# LOW-COVERAGE OBSERVATIONS — COMPLETION BATCH
Date: 2026-08-27
Status: ACTIVE

## O1. Public evidence can materially raise coverage, but the last 30-40% is structurally private/ground
The weak axes split into two classes.

### Publicly improvable
Human Psychology, Origin Mirror, Competitive Destination Mirror, 10Yx12M Temporal.
These can progress substantially through official surveys/statistics + commercial/public proxies + research.

### Structurally telemetry/ground dependent
Failure Journey, Real Sellable Supply, Ground Intelligence, Operational N-Telemetry and parts of Money Flow.
These require denominators, contracts, supplier state, or first-party events not normally published.

## O2. Money Flow must be a waterfall, not a single commission variable
Canonical chain:
`traveler total paid -> tax/refund -> FX/DCC -> payment fee -> platform fee/markup -> supplier payout -> operating cost -> contribution margin -> local retained value`.
Platform fee is `platform × country × product × contract × date`, not a universal percentage.

## O3. Psychology is a latent layer, not a label attached to nationality
Keep:
`underlying need != stated reason != search signal != purchase != realized behavior`.
Use multiple sensors and lag validation before upgrading a motive from HYPOTHESIS to supported latent state.

## O4. Origin mirror is necessary to distinguish Korea growth from global outbound growth
A rise in arrivals to Korea does not imply rising Korea preference if the origin country's total outbound market grew faster. Destination share/capture must be calculated against origin-market denominators using compatible definitions.

## O5. Competitive destinations require same-origin choice sets
Korea should be benchmarked against destinations competing for the same traveler/segment/month, not against a fixed list mechanically. Japan/Taiwan/Thailand/Singapore/Vietnam form the first Asia comparator backbone.

## O6. Temporal quality is limited by regime consistency, not just years of history
A 10-year file is not a 10-year comparable series if survey design, border policy, definition or sensor method changed. Regime IDs become first-class attributes.

## O7. Failure Journey is where latent demand becomes economically observable
`interest without realized transaction` can reflect no demand, or discoverability/language/booking/payment/mobility failure. Without attempt denominators these cannot be distinguished reliably.

## O8. Real Sellable Supply is a state machine
Official registration measures legal/known supply. Commercial opportunity needs state evidence through discoverability, language, booking, payment, reachability, willingness and capacity.

## O9. Ground Intelligence should be information-value targeted
Do not survey 100 suppliers equally. Desk-screen broadly, then call/site-validate cases where resolving UNKNOWN most changes opportunity ranking.

## O10. N-Telemetry is the future calibration moat
The first-party event contract creates denominators for search, booking, payment, move and experience failures. It is required before KTOF weights or failure-value estimates can be empirically trained.
