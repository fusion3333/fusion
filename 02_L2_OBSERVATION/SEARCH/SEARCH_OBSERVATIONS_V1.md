# SEARCH — L2 OBSERVATIONS V1
Date: 2026-08-28

1. SEARCH is not one stage. Inspiration, passive discovery, active query, comparison, map navigation and OTA transition are distinct states.
2. The largest public-data blind spot is not awareness but the middle funnel: result exposure -> click -> compare -> supplier/OTA transition -> booking attempt.
3. Social media is increasingly important as an inspiration sensor, but social influence cannot be equated with Korea-specific conversion without Korea/origin denominators.
4. Search indexes are useful for time/origin comparison only when normalization and query definitions are preserved.
5. A destination or experience can have high underlying demand and high-quality supply yet low realized visitation because discoverability and transaction bridges are weak.
6. Review quantity/quality and map/OTA presence are trust/discovery variables that can mediate conversion, but their causal contribution requires experiment or telemetry.
7. SEARCH must connect backward to WHY/psychology and forward to BOOK/PAY and MOVE; otherwise 'interest but no visit' cannot be diagnosed.
8. The key proprietary moat is attempted-but-failed behavior: searches returning poor results, result clicks without usable booking, map interest without reachable route, and booking abandonment.

## High-value derived candidates
- DISCOVERY_GAP: high interest + relevant supply + weak foreign-language/result visibility.
- COMPARISON_FRICTION: result exposure exists but trust/price/review/information comparability is weak.
- SEARCH_TO_BOOK_BRIDGE_GAP: supplier discovered but no usable booking path.
- SEARCH_TO_MOVE_GAP: place discovered but route/last-mile usability prevents conversion.

All are hypothesis/candidate labels until event denominators or controlled evidence exists.
