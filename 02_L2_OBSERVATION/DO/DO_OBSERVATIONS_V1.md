# DO — L2 OBSERVATIONS V1
Date: 2026-08-28
Status: EVIDENCE-SUPPORTED OBSERVATIONS

## O1. DO is a conversion layer, not a category list
The operational object is the conversion from desired/planned activities to completed activities. Survey, platform and research evidence support keeping these states separate.

`Desired -> Planned -> Discovered -> Booked -> Attempted -> Completed`

## O2. Activity demand is heterogeneous by origin and motive
Historical Korea inbound research and specialized KTO surveys show that activity preferences differ materially by origin and motive. Therefore DO must always be conditioned by WHO and WHY rather than ranked nationally as one universal list.

## O3. Regional tourism growth can be activity-led
Klook's owned data shows strong growth in local travel offerings/bookings outside Seoul. This supports an observation that regional demand can emerge around activities/experiences when discovery and access improve; it does not prove causality by rail or platform alone.

## O4. Experience authenticity is a measurable attribute gap
Social/VOC evidence repeatedly distinguishes authentic/local/small-business versions of traditional experiences from standardized/mass-produced versions. This should be captured as an attribute (`AUTHENTICITY_PERCEPTION`) rather than forcing all hanbok/hanok/craft experiences into a single category.

## O5. Activity feasibility depends on time, weather, mobility, booking and party context
Heat, child accompaniment, distance, operating hours and itinerary density repeatedly affect whether a desired activity is completed. DO therefore depends on WHEN, MOVE, BOOK/PAY, STAY and WHO.

## O6. A high-value metric is Planned-to-Actual Activity Conversion
Research using the Foreign Visitor Survey demonstrates a usable planned-versus-actual activity-conversion concept. The project should preserve this as a candidate metric family, with exact formulas validated only after raw-codebook harmonization.

## O7. Activity bundles are more valuable than isolated activity counts
Examples such as `HANBOK -> PALACE -> PHOTO -> TRADITIONAL_FOOD` or `TEMPLE -> TEA -> CRAFT -> HANOK` should be represented as co-occurrence/sequence patterns. This is the bridge from DO to Recombination/Revalue.

## O8. Missing activity completion is not automatically lack of interest
`Desired but not completed` may result from discoverability, booking, price, language, capacity, weather or last-mile friction. This is a direct bridge into Failure Journey.

## Candidate opportunity observations
- `HighIntent + LowActualParticipation + ExistingSupply` -> ActivityConversionGap candidate.
- `HighCompletion + HighSatisfaction + LowGlobalDistribution` -> Distribution/Revalue candidate.
- `HighActivityCoOccurrence + FragmentedBooking` -> Bundle/Itinerary product candidate.
- `HighRegionalVisit + LowPaidActivityDepth` -> Monetization-depth candidate.

All candidates remain hypotheses until denominators, supply state and conversion evidence are available.
