# WHY + HUMAN PSYCHOLOGY — L2 OBSERVATIONS V1
Date: 2026-08-28

These are evidence-status observations, not causal-effect claims.

- WHY is observable at multiple decision stages; one `reason_for_visit` field is insufficient.
- Potential demand and realized demand are different populations and must remain parallel nodes.
- Search interest is a bridge sensor, not conversion truth.
- Information influence is origin-market specific.
- K-content exposure remains upstream until conversion is observed.
- Negative/blocked intent is first-class evidence.

Rejected shortcuts: `K-content popularity = tourism demand`; `visit intent = future arrival`; `Google interest index = search volume`; `stated reason = causal effect size`; `no booking = no desire`.
