# SATISFACTION Observations V1

1. Korea has an official traveler-level post-trip satisfaction backbone, including overall/item satisfaction, revisit intention and recommendation intention.
2. SATISFACTION must be conditioned on WHO x WHY x WHEN x WHERE x STAY x DO x SPEND; aggregate averages can hide segment-specific failures.
3. PAIN and SATISFACTION are not inverses. A traveler can report high satisfaction after workarounds or successful service recovery.
4. Expectation is a reference point. The same delivered experience can generate different satisfaction for different origin/segment/motivation groups.
5. Stated loyalty is not revealed loyalty. Recommendation/revisit intention requires downstream telemetry or later-arrival linkage before being treated as behavior.
6. Platform ratings/reviews are valuable attribute and anomaly sensors but are selected samples, not population satisfaction estimates.
7. Opportunity signal: high demand + high completion + low attribute satisfaction can identify quality/value gaps; high satisfaction + low discoverability can identify under-distributed assets.
8. Service recovery should be modeled separately: failure -> recovery -> satisfaction can reveal where intervention preserves economic value.
