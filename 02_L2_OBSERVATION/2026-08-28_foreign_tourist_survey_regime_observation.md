# Foreign Tourist Survey regime observation — 2026-08-28

## Verified regime structure
- 2020–2022: COVID-era survey method/content changed or reduced.
- 2023: official survey returned approximately to the pre-COVID 2019 level.
- 2024–2025: official raw/codebook packages published.
- 2017: administration/location/target-market/sample changes.
- 2018: cruise travelers additionally excluded; age allocation added; stay eligibility changed from ≤1 year to ≤90 days; major regime break.
- 2019: survey naming and some geographic/question structures changed.
