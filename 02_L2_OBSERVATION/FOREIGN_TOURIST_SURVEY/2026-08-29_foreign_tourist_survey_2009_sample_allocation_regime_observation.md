## Verified observation

The official Foreign Tourist Survey history records two 2009 changes: survey administration moved from the Korea Tourism Organization to the Korea Culture & Tourism Institute, and the sample-allocation scheme added an age allocation dimension to the previously listed country/month/entry-route/sex dimensions.

This is a verified sample-design regime change, but it is not sufficient evidence for a raw-variable, code, weighting-formula, or longitudinal-equivalence claim.

## Official artifact stack

The current official registry verifies a same-year 2009 main codebook PDF, a country/city common codebook PDF, a 2009 questionnaire PDF, and a 2009 user-guide PDF. Their existence is VERIFIED_AVAILABLE.
