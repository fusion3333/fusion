# 2023 Foreign Tourist Survey — regime, sample design, and artifact observation

Date: 2026-08-29
Layer: L2 OBSERVATION
Scope: evidence-supported only

## Observation

2023 is a verified methodology transition out of the 2020–2022 COVID temporary survey regime. The official survey history states that, as COVID-19 influence decreased and inbound trends stabilized, the survey method and content were conducted at approximately the pre-COVID (2019) level. This must not be interpreted as proof that every 2019 variable, code, skip rule, or weight formula is identical in 2023.

The official 2023 survey-design page defines the target universe as foreign tourists aged 15 or older departing Korea after stays of 90 days or less. It explicitly excludes Korean-national foreigners, persons who only stayed overnight for transit, daigong traders, non-tourism pilots/crew/military personnel, and cruise travelers.

The planned annual sample is 16,000, allocated across 12 months at roughly 1,333 respondents per month (1,334 in some months). Allocation uses recent three-month inbound-tourism statistics in four stages: monthly allocation, modified proportional country allocation including square-root proportional allocation/minimum allocation, airport/port allocation, and country/sex/age allocation. This establishes restored sample design in 2023 but does not establish the exact respondent weight formula, calibration, strata, PSU, or nonresponse adjustment.

The official country-group frame lists Japan, China, Hong Kong, Singapore, Taiwan, Thailand, Malaysia, Australia, United States, Canada, United Kingdom, Germany, France, Russia, Middle East, India, Philippines, Indonesia, Vietnam, Mongolia, and Other. Exact raw codes and the membership definition of the Middle East group remain UNKNOWN.

Fieldwork locations are Incheon International Airport T1/T2, Gimpo, Gimhae, Jeju, Daegu and Yangyang international airports, plus Busan Port and Incheon Port international passenger terminals. Respondents complete the survey on tablet PCs in departure waiting areas; the official page states a 14-language survey system.

Official artifact coverage is now verified for the 2023 questionnaire PDF, codebook ZIP, final report PDF, and raw-data ZIP. The final report is registered as `2023 외래관광객조사 보고서(확정치)` on 2024-07-25. The questionnaire is registered on 2024-07-03 and the codebook ZIP on 2024-07-05. Raw respondent bytes are not ingested in this repository.

The publication regime is also verified: 2023 introduced quarterly provisional releases, followed by an annual final result. Provisional quarterly figures must not be silently substituted for annual final figures.
