# 2015 Foreign Tourist Survey — published method/regime observation

## Evidence boundary
Tier-A MCST/Korea Tourism Knowledge Information System evidence only. This observation does not substitute for same-year questionnaire/codebook/raw-data extraction.

## Verified observations
1. **Repeat-visit definition break at 2015 is explicit.** The official survey history states that visit-count reference changed from **recent 3 years** to **lifetime** in 2015. The MCST 2015 results release independently cautions that 2014 is recent-3-years whereas 2015 onward has no period limit. Therefore 2014 and 2015 repeat-visit values are not definition-compatible without an explicit regime break.
2. Under the 2015 regime, published visit-count shares are: first visit 53.9%, second 16.3%, third 7.9%, fourth-or-more 21.9%; the official published repeat-visitor share is 46.1%.
3. MCST reports a 2015 realized survey sample of **12,900** respondents (about 1,000 per month), surveyed at four international airports (Incheon, Jeju, Gimpo, Gimhae) and two international ports (Incheon, Busan) by interview.
4. Published average stay is **6.6 days**; average expenditure per person is **USD 1,712.5**; average daily expenditure is **USD 328.1**. The official release explicitly describes the daily metric as total expenditure divided by total stay duration. Exact respondent-level denominator/exclusion mechanics are not established by this release.
5. Published travel-type shares are individual 67.9%, group 26.2%, Air-tel 5.9%. The official report note warns that same-package group-tour respondents are capped at up to four depending on group size; consequently these published travel-type shares are **not the population proportions of all inbound visitors**.
