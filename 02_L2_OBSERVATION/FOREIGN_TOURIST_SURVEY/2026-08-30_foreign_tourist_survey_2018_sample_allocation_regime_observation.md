## Verified observation

2018 is a substantive survey-design boundary, not merely a new annual wave. Official KTO material verifies all of the following same-year changes: Mongolian tourists were newly added; the planned sample scale increased from the prior 13,841 level to 16,000; cruise travelers were newly excluded from the survey population; age was added to the pre-existing country/month/entry-route/sex sample-allocation dimensions; Incheon International Airport Terminal 2 was added as a field site; and the eligible stay ceiling was shortened from up to one year to 90 days or less.

The realized 2018 sample was 16,469. Published monthly realized counts were Jan 1,394; Feb 1,390; Mar 1,380; Apr 1,372; May 1,366; Jun 1,358; Jul 1,353; Aug 1,327; Sep 1,398; Oct 1,367; Nov 1,367; Dec 1,397. These sum to the published annual realized sample and provide verified monthly coverage, but not respondent-level weighting or response-rate information.

2018 also marks a publication-regime change: KTO added quarterly provisional releases while retaining the annual final release. Provisional quarterly figures must therefore not be silently substituted for annual final values without checking revision status.

The official survey overview verifies use of self-completion paper questionnaires or on-site face-to-face interviews and a 12-language questionnaire set: English, Japanese, Simplified Chinese, Traditional Chinese, Thai, German, French, Russian, Filipino, Vietnamese, Indonesian and Mongolian.

## Evidence-safe interpretation

These facts strengthen the already-known 2018 comparability warning. Longitudinal analysis crossing 2017→2018 must preserve the simultaneous changes in survey universe, allocation dimensions, country frame, field-site frame and stay eligibility. No correction factor or harmonized equivalence is constructed here.
