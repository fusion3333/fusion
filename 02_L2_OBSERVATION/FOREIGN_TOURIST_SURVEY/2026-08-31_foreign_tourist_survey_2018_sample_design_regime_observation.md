# 2018 Foreign Tourist Survey — Sample Design and Regime Observation

Status: L2 OBSERVATION / EVIDENCE-SUPPORTED ONLY
Date: 2026-08-31
Canonical dataset id: `KORC-FTS-001`

## Observation

A Tier-A official Korea Tourism Organization publication page provides same-year 2018 survey-design evidence that materially narrows the 2018 regime boundary beyond artifact availability alone.

The page distinguishes a design-history expansion from 13,841 to 16,000 respondents from the reported achieved 2018 survey size of 16,469 respondents (approximately 1,372 per month). These are preserved as different quantities rather than treated as a conflict.

The 2018 regime also changed in multiple dimensions: Mongolian tourists were newly added; cruise travelers were newly excluded in addition to overseas Koreans and crew; sample allocation added age to the previously stated country × month × entry-route × sex dimensions; Incheon International Airport Terminal 2 was added as a survey site; and the eligible stay-period ceiling was reduced from one year to 90 days.

The official page also documents a publication-regime change from annual final results only to additional quarterly provisional releases, and states that data collection used paper self-completion or on-site face-to-face question-and-answer. A 12-language questionnaire system was used according to respondent language area, but the page's visible language list is not treated as an independently verified exhaustive version inventory.

## Comparability boundary

The 2018 population, allocation, fieldwork-site and stay-eligibility changes constitute a same-year regime break. Pre-2018 respondent populations and stay-related measures must not be pooled mechanically with 2018 merely because variable labels appear similar.

Sample-allocation dimensions are not promoted to weighting/calibration dimensions without direct weighting documentation. Likewise, the official topic inventory confirms that constructs existed in the 2018 instrument but does not establish raw variable names, code values, universes/skips, missing codes or longitudinal equivalence.
