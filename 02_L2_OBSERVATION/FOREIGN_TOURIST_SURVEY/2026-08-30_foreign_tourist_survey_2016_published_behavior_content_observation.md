The official table marks destination-choice factors and information sources as multiple-response measures. ## Evidence boundary
These are published aggregate results, not respondent-level field extraction. They therefore improve evidence coverage of WHY/SEARCH/WHO/SATISFACTION content but do not close the canonical P0 field-extraction gate.

Do not infer from these aggregates:

- complete response-code sets;
- exact questionnaire wording;
- skip/universe rules;
- item-specific missing codes;
- respondent-level FIT derivation;
- weighting construction or item-specific denominators.

All such fields remain `UNKNOWN/PENDING` until the 2016 same-year codebook/user guide/raw artifact can be directly extracted.

The next material P0 advance is therefore direct extraction of the official 2016 codebook/user guide or gated raw package, not adjacent-year inference.
