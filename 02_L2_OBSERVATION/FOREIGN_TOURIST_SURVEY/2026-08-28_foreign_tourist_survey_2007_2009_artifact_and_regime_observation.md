# Foreign Tourist Survey 2007–2009 official artifact and regime observation

Date: 2026-08-28
Evidence lane: official survey harmonization only
Confidence: HIGH for artifact existence and stated survey-history regime breaks; UNKNOWN for field-level harmonization until files are inspected

## Verified evidence

The Tourism Knowledge Information System currently lists respondent-level raw downloads for all three years 2007, 2008 and 2009. The listed filenames are `2007년 외래관광객 실태조사 원자료.zip`, `2008년 외래관광객 실태조사 원자료.zip`, and `2009년 외래관광객 실태조사 원자료.zip`. These downloads are behind the portal's official data-application form.

The official codebook index also lists a year-specific codebook for 2007 and 2008. For 2009 it lists both the main survey codebook and a separate common country/city codebook. The official questionnaire index lists downloadable PDFs for 2007, 2008 and 2009.

The current official user-guide index begins with a 2009 Foreign Tourist Survey user guide. No 2007 or 2008 user-guide entries were verified on that current list. This must be represented as `NOT_VERIFIED_ON_CURRENT_LIST`, not as proof that such guides never existed.

## Verified regime breaks

The official survey-history page states that in 2008 Indian visitors were added to the survey and the sample expanded from 11,453 to 11,978. Therefore 2007 and 2008 must not be treated as an unchanged population/weighting regime without direct codebook and raw-file comparison.

The same official history states that in 2009 survey administration changed from the Korea Tourism Organization to the Korea Culture and Tourism Institute. This is a provenance and methodology regime marker. It does not by itself prove a measurement discontinuity, but the 2009 questionnaire/codebook/raw should be crosswalked directly against 2008 rather than inferred from adjacent years.

## Evidence consequence

The accessible official artifact horizon for the Foreign Tourist Survey is now directly verified back to 2007 for raw respondent data, codebooks and questionnaires. This removes the previous need to treat 2007–2009 as historical-report-only years.

This does **not** complete harmonization. Remaining work is field-level inspection and crosswalk of variable names, question wording, response codes, residence-country/city codes, weights, sample design, stay definitions and expenditure definitions across all accessible waves. No coverage score or P0 completion status should be increased solely from artifact existence.
