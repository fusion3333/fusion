# Foreign Tourist Survey 2007–2015 supporting-artifact coverage observation

Status: EVIDENCE-SUPPORTED L2 OBSERVATION
Date: 2026-08-29
Dataset: `KORC-FTS-001`

## Observation
The current official KCTI Tourism Knowledge Information System registries materially close several stale supporting-artifact gaps in the canonical 2007–2025 manifest.

- Annual official reports are directly listed for every survey year 2007–2015.
- Annual official questionnaire PDFs are directly listed for every survey year 2007–2015.
- Annual official user guides are directly listed for 2009–2015. No 2007–2008 user-guide entry is visible on the current official registry; this is retained as NOT_VERIFIED rather than treated as proof of non-publication.
- Separate country/city codebook artifacts are directly listed for 2009, 2010, 2013, 2014 and 2015. The 2009 item is labelled a common country/city codebook. 2011–2012 country/city code availability remains unverified in this run.

## Canonical impact
This evidence supports mechanical artifact-availability promotion only. It does not complete the P0 variable crosswalk and does not establish longitudinal equivalence.

The following remain pending unless separately supported by same-year extraction: raw variable names, exact wording, code values, variable-specific universe/filter, units, weights and formulas, missing codes, geography mappings, stay criterion, expenditure basis and sample-design details.

## Comparability safeguards
No artifact existence is interpreted as proof that adjacent-year questionnaires, code frames, geography maps or weighting rules are equivalent. The known 2011 eligible-stay expansion, 2013/2014 visit-place changes and 2015 repeat-visit definition break remain separate regime/comparability markers.
