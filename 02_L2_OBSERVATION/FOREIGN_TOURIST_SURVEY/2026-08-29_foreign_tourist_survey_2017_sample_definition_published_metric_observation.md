## Verified observations

1. The 2017 official survey overview defines the eligible population as age 15+, staying 1 day to 1 year. It excludes foreign visitors holding Korean nationality, transit visitors without domestic tourism, and uniformed pilots/crew/military unless in civilian clothes and visiting for tourism.
2. Respondent selection was constrained within travel parties: one respondent among family or groups of 9 or fewer, and up to two for groups of 10+ with different sex/age groups.
3. 2017 fieldwork sites were Incheon, Gimpo, Gimhae, Jeju, Cheongju and Daegu airports plus Busan and Incheon ports. Daegu and Cheongju were newly added in 2017 to improve representativeness.
4. The official mode was a structured questionnaire administered on site either face-to-face or by self-completion.
5. Realized sample was 13,841, averaging 1,153 per month. The official release describes the country frame as 19 countries plus Other and explicitly says Vietnam, Indonesia and the Philippines were added relative to 2016.
6. Published 2017 visit-frequency categories were 1 visit 46.7%, 2+ visits 53.3%, 2 visits 17.9%, 3 visits 9.5%, and 4+ visits 25.9%. These are published results, not raw code values.
7. Published average stay was 7.0 days, with the published metric explicitly excluding stays of 61 days or longer. This must not be misread as the survey eligibility ceiling, which remained 1 year in the official overview.
8. Published expenditure metrics were USD 1,481.6 per person overall and USD 270.4 per person-day overall. The release does not expose the raw expenditure component construction, exchange-rate rule, outlier treatment, or daily denominator formula.
9. Published overall satisfaction 94.8% is explicitly aggregated as `매우 만족 + 만족` (42.6% + 52.2%).
10. Published revisit intention is explicitly framed as tourism-purpose revisit within the next 3 years; 85.7% is the positive aggregation of `매우 그렇다` 37.9% + `그렇다` 47.8%.
11. Published recommendation intention 90.0% is likewise the aggregation of `매우 그렇다` 37.7% + `그렇다` 52.3%.
