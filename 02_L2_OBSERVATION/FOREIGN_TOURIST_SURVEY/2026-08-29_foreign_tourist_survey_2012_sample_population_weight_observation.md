## Verified observations

1. The 2012 same-year official report defines the survey population as foreigners age 15+ staying in Korea for at least 1 day and no more than 1 year, departing through Incheon, Gimpo, Gimhae or Jeju international airports or Incheon/Busan international ports. Transit-only overnight cases and captains/crew/military in uniform are excluded; the report explicitly notes that plain-clothes visitors whose purpose is tourism are eligible.

2. The official 2012 population frame uses January–December inbound-tourism statistics. The report states a population total of **9,803,439**, based on total arrivals after excluding overseas Koreans and crew.

3. The annual target sample was **12,000** (about 1,000 per month) and the realized valid sample was **12,021**. These must remain distinct.

4. The official annual allocation table reports population share, target sample and realized sample for 17 residence groups: Japan, China, Hong Kong, Singapore, Taiwan, Thailand, Malaysia, Australia, United States, Canada, United Kingdom, Germany, France, Russia, Middle East, India and Other. This verifies the same-year published residence-group frame, but not the constituent-country definitions of Middle East or Other.

5. The same-year sample-characteristics table directly reports unweighted realized monthly counts: Jan 980, Feb 1,008, Mar 1,009, Apr 1,012, May 1,012, Jun 1,008, Jul 1,000, Aug 988, Sep 1,020, Oct 990, Nov 1,008 and Dec 986, totaling 12,021.

6. The official report states that **all published analyses use weighted cases** and separately displays weighted versus unweighted distributions by residence country and survey month. This verifies that an analysis weight was applied in 2012. It does not reveal the raw weight variable name or full formula from the currently retrievable indexed text.

7. Survey history records 2012 as the year the survey was selected for construction in Statistics Korea's Nara Statistics system. Treat this as a provenance/system regime marker only, not as proof of a measurement break.

## Comparability restraint

Do not infer the 2012 raw weight variable, exact calibration cells, nonresponse adjustment, questionnaire wording, raw codes or skip/universe rules from 2011 or 2013. The same-year report is sufficient to reduce population/sample/weight-use UNKNOWNs, but not to establish field equivalence.
