## Verified observations

1. The official 2025 Foreign Tourist Survey task specification requires a planned sample of at least 16,000 respondents, approximately 1,333 per month, from foreigners departing after visiting Korea across the 20-country-group-plus-Other survey frame.

2. The same-year RFP identifies nationality, sex, entry point and age as dimensions to be considered in monthly sample allocation. The final allocation formula, realized allocation cells, weight variable, calibration, nonresponse adjustment and sample-design identifiers remain unverified and must not be inferred from 2024.

3. The official 2025 RFP specifies a 14-language survey set: English, Japanese, Simplified Chinese, Traditional Chinese, French, German, Russian, Thai, Vietnamese, Indonesian, Filipino, Mongolian, Arabic and Malaysian.

4. The official 2024 procurement specification described a 12-language set without Arabic or Malaysian. Therefore a planned survey-language-set difference is verified for 2025. This is a design/fieldwork routing break only; no response-measurement effect or questionnaire-wording change is inferred.

5. The 2025 task specification contractually requires delivery to the commissioning body of raw and processed data, codebook, user guide, analysis syntax, fieldwork report/manual, data-editing report including nonresponse imputation, sample-design report including weights, reliability report and related deliverables. This proves required internal deliverables, not public availability.

6. The official Korea Tourism Data Lab publicly lists the 2025 raw ZIP and codebook XLSX. The current official Foreign Tourist Survey questionnaire registry still has no 2025 questionnaire entry, so public questionnaire status remains `UNKNOWN/NOT_VERIFIED`, not `NOT_PUBLISHED_BY_DESIGN`.

## Evidence boundary / blockers

The 2025 XLSX codebook itself still needs direct field-level extraction. `raw_variable`, exact wording, complete code values, universe/filter, missing codes, final weight variable/formula, final realized monthly/annual sample, final language actually used per respondent, country/visit-place mappings, expenditure transforms and respondent raw rows remain `UNKNOWN/PENDING` unless separately evidenced.

The procurement specification must not be substituted for the final implemented questionnaire, final sample-design report or final raw-data schema where those could differ during execution.
