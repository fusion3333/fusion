# PLATFORM Observations V1

## O1 Platform is not a channel flag
A supplier can be present on multiple platform families with different discovery, transaction, customer-ownership and support roles. Store platform presence as platform x product x traveler-market x date.

## O2 Distribution has at least four control points
1. discovery/ranking control
2. trust/reputation control
3. transaction/merchant-of-record control
4. post-booking/customer-relationship control
A platform may control all four or only a subset.

## O3 Visibility can have an economic price
Viator Accelerate is verified evidence that additional commission can increase advertising exposure, alongside popularity and review credibility. Therefore ranking/exposure must not be modeled as purely organic quality.

## O4 Platform reach and supplier economics can oppose each other
A platform can improve foreign-language discovery, payment and support while simultaneously reducing supplier retained value through commission, promotions, affiliate payouts, payment/FX or operational requirements. This tradeoff belongs between REAL_SELLABLE_SUPPLY and MONEY_FLOW.

## O5 Customer ownership is architecture-dependent
Merchant-of-record, customer-data ownership and customer support differ by integration model even within the same platform ecosystem. These must be stored as transaction-role attributes rather than inferred from brand.

## O6 Social platforms are upstream distribution infrastructure
Social channels can create inspiration, social proof and referral traffic but public posts do not provide a reliable denominator for population conversion. Store social as discovery/referral/VOC sensors unless first-party attribution is available.

## O7 Platform Island candidate
High-quality supply + low global-platform coverage + high target-market demand is a Distribution Island candidate, not automatically an opportunity. Check direct-channel sellability, supplier willingness, capacity and economics.

## O8 Platform Dependency candidate
High platform booking share + low direct repeat capture + high effective distribution cost + weak customer ownership is a Platform Dependency candidate. Requires supplier Ground data or partner dashboard data before scoring.

## O9 Ranking Tax candidate
When incremental visibility requires higher commission or paid placement, the marginal acquisition cost of visibility must be separated from base commission. Do not generalize one platform mechanism to all platforms.

## O10 Cross-platform identity resolution is mandatory
The same supplier/product may have different names, languages, prices, cancellation terms, review histories and inventory across platforms. Entity resolution must occur before coverage, price or competition comparisons.
