# MONEY_FLOW tax-refund regime-break observation — 2026-08-28

Confidence: HIGH for the policy dates/rules; UNKNOWN for behavioral/economic effect.

## Evidence-supported observation
The foreign-tourist retail internal-tax refund environment is not temporally stationary.

Two verified policy/operational breaks must be preserved in longitudinal data:
1. **2024-01-01 threshold regime break** — official legal revision history records higher purchase caps for immediate refund and downtown refund.
2. **2026-04-06 access-population regime break** — Korea Customs Service extended immediate/downtown refund access to cruise tourists using the tourist landing-permit process by linking Customs data to the refund system.

## Required evidence handling
Any 10Y/12M series involving tax-refund eligibility, channel availability, refund usage, or associated shopping spend must carry an effective-date/regime field. Pre/post values should not be interpreted as behavior change without controlling for the rule change.

## What is NOT observed
No causal claim is made that either reform increased spend, refund use, conversion, satisfaction, or local retained value. Those outcomes require transaction/survey evidence with appropriate denominators.
