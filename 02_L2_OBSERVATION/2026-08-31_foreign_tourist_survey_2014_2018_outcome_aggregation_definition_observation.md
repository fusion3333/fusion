# Foreign Tourist Survey 2014–2018 published outcome aggregation definition observation

Date verified: 2026-08-31
Evidence class: Official / Tier-A

## Verified observation
Official Korea Tourism Organization retrospective publications explicitly define how several published Foreign Tourist Survey outcome percentages were constructed.

- Overall satisfaction in the 2014–2018 published series is the sum of `매우 만족` + `대체로 만족`. The official table reports total values of 94.0% (2014), 93.5% (2015), 95.0% (2016), 94.8% (2017), and 93.1% (2018).
- Tourism revisit intention within the next three years is the sum of `매우 그렇다` + `대체로 그렇다`. The official table reports 85.4% (2014), 85.6% (2015), 84.1% (2016), 85.7% (2017), and 84.4% (2018).
- The 2019 final report explicitly states the same positive-response construction for recommendation intention and gives the 2018 comparator as 88.0%.

## Coverage change
Existing 2017 field-content evidence had the positive-response aggregation rule for revisit/recommendation marked UNKNOWN. The retrospective Tier-A publication now verifies the published-metric aggregation rule for revisit intention, and the 2019 final-report comparator verifies the published 2018 recommendation metric construction.

## Boundary
This is a published-metric definition bridge, not a raw-field harmonization bridge. It does **not** establish identical raw variable names, code numbers, questionnaire wording, weights, universe/skip logic, denominator exclusions, or missing-value handling across 2014–2018. Same-year codebook/questionnaire/raw artifacts remain authoritative for respondent-level longitudinal crosswalk work.

## Remaining blocker
Direct same-year extraction is still required for raw variable names, full five-point code frames, universe/skip, item missing, denominator construction and respondent-level validation, especially for 2017–2018 HWP codebooks/raw files.
