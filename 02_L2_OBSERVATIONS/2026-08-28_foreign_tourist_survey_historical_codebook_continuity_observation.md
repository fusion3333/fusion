# Foreign Tourist Survey historical codebook continuity observation — 2026-08-28

## Evidence scope
Official Korea Tourism Data Lab codebook artifacts were independently verified for 2010, 2011, 2013 and 2014. This observation does not assert that the corresponding respondent-level raw files were obtained. 2012 remains unverified in this run.

## Evidence-supported observations
1. A longitudinal variable-crosswalk can extend materially earlier than the currently verified 2020+ raw-wave set because official historical codebooks are still exposed by Korea Tourism Data Lab.
2. The 2010 codebook explicitly contains survey-month, residence-country, demographic, weight, travel-form, visit-purpose, alternative-destination and travel-motivation variables.
3. The 2011 codebook explicitly contains travel form, repeat-visit history, stay length, decision timing, alternative-country consideration, visit purpose and travel motivation.
4. The 2013 codebook explicitly contains survey-month, residence-country, demographic and weight fields.
5. The 2014 codebook explicitly contains survey-month, residence-country, demographic, weight and daily-average expenditure raw variables.

## Non-inference rules
- Codebook availability != respondent raw-data availability.
- Same concept label across years != identical question wording, universe, code values or methodology.
- Missing verification for 2012 != absence of a 2012 survey or codebook.
- Historical expenditure variables must not be concatenated with later-wave expenditure fields until currency, inclusions/exclusions, travel-type treatment and derived-variable formulas are verified year by year.
- Monthly survey-period variables permit month-tagging of responses; they do not by themselves prove that every published estimate is designed or powered for month-level inference.

## Remaining blockers
- Authoritative 2012 artifact metadata remains UNKNOWN.
- Respondent-level raw-file availability and license metadata for 2010-2014 were not independently verified in this run.
- Full variable-by-variable wording/code/value crosswalk across 2010-2025 remains pending.

## Confidence
HIGH for existence/content of the cited historical codebooks; UNKNOWN/PENDING for raw-file availability and full longitudinal comparability.
