# ARCHITECTURE V2 — TOURISM META-INTELLIGENCE & OPPORTUNITY OS
Date: 2026-08-27
Status: ADOPTED DIRECTION

## Mission
한국 관광 데이터를 많이 모으는 것이 목적이 아니다.
기존 전문 Intelligence와 공식/기업/Social/Research/Ground evidence를 연결해, 한국 관광산업의 미연결 가치와 해결 가능한 사업기회를 발견하고 실제 실험·거래 결과로 학습하는 운영체계를 만든다.

## Core equation (conceptual, NOT final scoring formula)
Existing Intelligence + Korea Official Data + Enterprise Data + Social/VOC + Research + Proprietary Ground Truth + Transaction Outcomes → Meta Intelligence → Opportunity Discovery → Business Experiment → Learning.

## Seven layers
L0 SOURCE & EXISTING ENGINE: KTO/정부/지역기관/ForwardKeys/Data Appeal/AirDNA/OTA/card/telecom/maps/social/research/suppliers.
L1 EVIDENCE ADAPTER: source class, measurement type, definition, time, geography, methodology, license, bias, provenance, confidence.
L2 CANONICAL MEASUREMENT: visitor, search, booking, ticket, mobility, spend, POI, supply, price, review, sentiment, capacity etc. Never equate census/survey/transaction/proxy/model-derived.
L3 TEMPORAL KNOWLEDGE GRAPH: Traveler Lifecycle × Place × Supplier × Product × Platform × Transaction × Information × Trust × Event/Regime.
L4 FRICTION & MISSINGNESS: planned/actual/desired-but-failed; drop-off; UNKNOWN/CONFLICTING; Island/Bottleneck/Asymmetry/Value Leakage.
L5 GROUND VALIDATION: Real Sellable Supply, capacity, language reality, foreign payment, willingness, packaging/repricing/collaboration feasibility.
L6 OPPORTUNITY & EXPERIMENT: hypothesis ranking, recombination, revalue, experiment design. No unvalidated final KTOF.
L7 TRANSACTION/OUTCOME: click, inquiry, booking, payment, cancellation, margin, satisfaction, repeat/revisit → calibration feedback.

## Moat
Not raw data ownership alone. Moat = cross-engine semantic normalization + proprietary Korean ground truth + valuable-missingness research loop + opportunity-to-transaction feedback dataset.

## Research Engine
For every candidate decision compute conceptually:
Missing Evidence × Decision Sensitivity × Source Availability × Acquisition Cost.
This is a research-priority hypothesis, not a finalized equation. The engine asks what evidence should be collected next to reduce decision uncertainty most efficiently.

## Opportunity policy
An empty cell is not automatically an opportunity. Candidate requires evidence of demand/value plus solvable friction and viable economics. `UNKNOWN != ZERO` and `GAP != OPPORTUNITY` are hard rules.

## Competitor positioning
Data Appeal/Mabrian proves multi-source destination intelligence and agentic insight are becoming commoditized. ForwardKeys proves aviation journey intelligence is specialized and deep. AirDNA proves domain-specific scoring can be strong. KTO Data Lab provides Korea-specific mobility/spend/navigation primitives. Therefore differentiation moves one layer above: cross-engine opportunity operating system with ground/transaction feedback.

## Immediate execution order
1. Method Library (public formulas/methods/unknowns).
2. Primitive-to-Variable mapping against Traveler Lifecycle ontology.
3. Build/Buy/Connect matrix for each variable.
4. O1 official Korean raw-data ingestion, 10y+ where available.
5. Enterprise/Social evidence only where it adds a distinct measurement.
6. Generate UNKNOWN/CONFLICTING/low-confidence map.
7. Ground Research Queue for variables unavailable online.
8. First opportunity hypotheses only after evidence gates.
9. Run small supplier/customer experiments and capture outcome labels.
10. Estimate/validate KTOF only after sufficient outcome data.

## Success criterion
The system succeeds when it can answer, with traceable evidence: `Where is valuable unmet/misconnected tourism demand, why does it exist, who can solve it, what must be changed, what is the expected economic value, what evidence is missing, and what experiment should we run next?`
