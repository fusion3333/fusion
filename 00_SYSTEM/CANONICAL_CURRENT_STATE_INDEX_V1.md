## Source classes — fixed meanings
- O = Official Data: data produced/held/published by governments, public agencies, official statistical/administrative bodies.
- E = Enterprise Data: data directly produced/held by enterprises through business operations, including transaction, booking, inventory, mobility, airline, payment and platform operational data. E must not be broadened into generic 'market data'.
- S = Social Data: user-generated social/internet/platform expression and behavioral-expression data such as reviews, communities, SNS, blogs, YouTube and search/social signals.
- R = Research Data: data, empirical results and methods produced by universities, research institutes and scholarly research.
- G = Ground Data: first-party field evidence actively collected by this project from the real business/physical field, including supplier interviews, observations, actual prices, contracts, settlement statements, costs, capacity and mystery transactions.
- N = Native Data: behavioral and transaction telemetry generated natively by operation of our own service/system, including search, impression, click, booking attempt, payment attempt, success/failure, refund, repeat and experiments.

O/E/S/R are external-world evidence classes; G is evidence we actively create by measuring the field; N is evidence generated by operation of our own product/system. They are fit-for-purpose sensors, not a single credibility ladder.
