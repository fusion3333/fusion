## PUBLIC BOUNDARY / NOT A FAILURE OF THE WORKSTREAM
The following require non-public commercial, accounting, Ground or owned telemetry: negotiated OTA commission, supplier-funded promotion, affiliate incidence, realized PG/acquirer/network fee, effective FX/DCC spread, refund-operator deduction, merchant-level tax incidence, refund/chargeback loss, payout timing cost, labor/variable cost, local input sourcing, supplier operating profit, CAC/repeat economics and transaction-level local retained value.

These fields must remain NOT_PUBLIC / COMMERCIAL_RESTRICTED / GROUND_REQUIRED / N_TELEMETRY_REQUIRED until evidence exists. They must not be estimated from generic industry averages for empirical scoring.

## Reopen rule
Reopen only for materially new official transaction datasets, material fee/tax/platform-rule changes, commercial partner access, supplier accounting evidence or owned telemetry.
