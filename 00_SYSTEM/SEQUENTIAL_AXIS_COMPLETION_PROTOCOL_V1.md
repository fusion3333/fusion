# SEQUENTIAL AXIS COMPLETION PROTOCOL V1

Date: 2026-08-27
Status: ACTIVE MASTER

## Purpose
From this point, korea-tour stops broad parallel expansion as the default. Each axis is completed as far as evidence permits before moving to the next axis. The objective is not to force 100% completeness, but to maximize verified evidence, expose irreducible UNKNOWNs, and lock a stable measurement/graph contract for each axis.

## Source classes
Every axis uses the established routing classes:
- O = Official / government / public authority
- E = Enterprise / platform / operator / commercial data
- S = Social / review / VOC / community
- R = Research / academic / international organization / specialist research
- G = Ground intelligence / interviews / field validation
- N = Operational / first-party telemetry / partner logs

No axis may be declared complete based on one class alone when the phenomenon requires multiple independent sensors.

## Completion pipeline
For each axis:
1. Question/Variable inventory freeze
2. Measurement-type definitions freeze
3. O/E/S/R/G/N source-priority routing
4. Official backbone acquisition and methodology audit
5. Enterprise/platform augmentation
6. Social/VOC taxonomy and representative evidence
7. Research triangulation and causal/behavioral framing
8. Ground/Telemetry missingness boundary
9. 10+ year × monthly/seasonal availability audit where meaningful
10. Korea vs origin-country mirror mapping where meaningful
11. Competitive-destination mirror mapping where meaningful
12. L1 evidence pack
13. L2 observations
14. L3 graph/state-transition mapping
15. UNKNOWN / CONFLICTING / PROPRIETARY / UNOBSERVABLE registry
16. Coverage score and confidence audit
17. Axis LOCK if completion gate satisfied; otherwise BLOCKED with explicit next acquisition requirement
18. Move to next axis only after LOCK/BLOCKED decision

## Axis completion gate
An axis can be LOCKED only when:
- Canonical variables and denominators are explicit.
- Highest-authority available sources are verified.
- Material methodology breaks are preserved.
- Major O/E/S/R perspectives are represented when relevant.
- Ground/N requirements are identified rather than guessed.
- At least one independent triangulation path exists for high-value claims.
- 10-year/monthly requirement is met where genuinely available, or structural unavailability is documented.
- Korea-origin/competitor mirrors are mapped when the axis depends on destination choice.
- No unresolved definition conflict can silently corrupt the graph.
- Remaining gaps are tagged by type: MISSING / UNKNOWN / PROPRIETARY / GROUND_REQUIRED / N_REQUIRED.

## Completion score is multidimensional
Do not use one percentage alone. Track:
- Source Coverage
- Sensor Diversity
- Temporal Depth
- Geographic Coverage
- Segment Granularity
- Definition/Methodology Quality
- Cross-validation Strength
- Engine-ready Coverage

Public-evidence completeness and engine-ready completeness must remain separate.

## Non-negotiables
- Never fabricate missing monthly or segment detail.
- Never convert survey intent into actual behavior without state labeling.
- Never equate registered supply with sellable supply.
- Never equate search, booking, payment, movement, spend, or review as substitutes.
- Never let an AI/derived estimate overwrite an administrative or direct observation.
- Never finalize opportunity weights before outcome data exists.
- If an axis cannot be completed with public data, mark the exact Ground/N acquisition requirement and lock the public layer rather than endlessly searching.

## Operational reporting
Every completed axis must finish with:
- what was acquired,
- what changed,
- what remains unknown,
- what moved from hypothesis to observation,
- what was rejected/superseded,
- current public-evidence coverage,
- current engine-ready coverage,
- next axis.
