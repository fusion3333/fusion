## 1. Core rule
Every project answer, collection plan, normalization, graph join and opportunity judgment must route the question to the source class and measurement type that most directly measures the phenomenon. There is no universal source prestige ladder.

`QUESTION → VARIABLE → MEASUREMENT → SOURCE ROUTER → NORMALIZATION → TRIANGULATION → UNCERTAINTY → GRAPH → GAP/OPPORTUNITY → INTERVENTION/OUTCOME`

## 2. Canonical Source Classes

### O — Official Data
Data produced, held or officially published by national/local governments, public agencies, official statistics/administrative bodies and comparable foreign official institutions.

Typical uses: population/universe, arrivals, policy, regulation, licensing, official supply, macro conditions, official surveys.

### E — Enterprise Data
Data directly produced or held by enterprises through business operations.

Examples: issuer/acquirer approvals and spend, OTA search/book/cancel/inventory, telecom mobility, airline booking, PG/payment, platform transactions, supplier operational systems.

Typical uses: actual price, inventory, booking, payment, transaction, operational availability, distribution and conversion.

**E is not a synonym for generic 'market data'.** Public web pages can be evidence about an enterprise, but classification as E depends on the underlying data-generation process being enterprise operational data.

### S — Social Data
User-generated social/internet/platform expression and behavioral-expression data.

Examples: Google reviews, Reddit, SNS, blogs, YouTube, community VOC, public review platforms and user-generated search/social signals where lawful to use.

Typical uses: pain, wants, trust, dissatisfaction, language expression, hidden needs, social diffusion and emerging trends.

### R — Research Data
Data, empirical findings, methods and research outputs produced by universities, research institutes and scholarly research.

Typical uses: causal mechanisms, WTP methods, tourism behavior, international comparison, methodology, prior empirical evidence.

### G — Ground Data
First-party field evidence actively created by this project by measuring the real business/physical field.

Examples: supplier interviews, field observation, actual prices, contract terms, settlement statements, labor/variable costs, capacity/utilization, mystery shopping/booking/payment, foreign-language response tests, realized OTA commission.

G fills reality that O/E/S/R cannot reliably expose at the required unit of analysis.

### N — Native Data
Behavioral and transaction evidence generated natively by operation of our own service/system.

Canonical funnel:
`Search → Impression → Click → Detail → Book Attempt → Payment Attempt → Success/Failure → Experience → Refund → Review → Repeat`

Examples: event telemetry, booking/payment outcomes, abandonment, refunds, repeat, experiment assignment, actual revenue and settlement linkage.

## 3. Relationship among classes
O/E/S/R are evidence produced in the external world by official institutions, enterprises, society/platform users and research. G is evidence we actively create by measuring the field. N is evidence generated by operation of our own product/system.

They are different sensors for different realities, not a single credibility ladder.

Canonical interpretation:
O measures institutional and official reality; E measures enterprise operational market/transaction reality; S measures social expression and reaction; R tests relationships and mechanisms through research; G measures field reality invisible to those sources; N continuously measures actual behavior and transactions inside our system.

## 4. Measurement types
At minimum preserve:
CENSUS/ADMIN, SURVEY, TRANSACTION, BOOKING, MOBILITY, SEARCH, NAVIGATION, PRICE/INVENTORY, REVIEW, SOCIAL, SUPPLY_REGISTRY, MODEL_DERIVED, RESEARCH_ESTIMATE, FIELD_OBSERVATION, CONTRACT/SETTLEMENT, NATIVE_EVENT, EXPERIMENT_OUTCOME.

`Observed Population != Sample != Transaction != Booking != Search Proxy != Social Signal != Model Output`

## 5. Required provenance
For important evidence preserve where applicable:
`source_id, canonical_dataset_id, source_class(O/E/S/R/G/N), source_priority, owner/publisher, dataset/product/engine, original_url/file, acquired_at, publication_date, period_start/end, frequency, geography, population/sample/universe, measurement_type, definition, unit, methodology_regime, sample_size/data_volume, granularity, license/terms, access restriction, known_bias, limitations, confidence, missingness_status, comparability_status, raw_or_derived, parent_source_id, graph_node/edge`.

## 6. Missingness and comparability
UNKNOWN is not zero. Preserve explicit states such as NOT_PUBLIC, NOT_VERIFIED, NOT_COMPARABLE, REGIME_BREAK, ACCESS_CONSTRAINED, COMMERCIAL_RESTRICTED, GROUND_REQUIRED and N_TELEMETRY_REQUIRED.

Never silently splice methodology regimes or substitute nationality for residence, survey spend for card spend, registered supply for sellable supply, complaint count for population failure rate, or published fee for realized fee.

## 7. Current architecture coverage
This standard applies to all 25 base axes and 8 cross-layers. Older 18-axis routing is historical.

The 25-axis frame covers WHO, WHY/psychology, WHEN, origin markets, competitive destinations, SEARCH, BOOK/PAY, MOVE, STAY, DO, SPEND, PAIN, SATISFACTION/REPEAT, SUPPLY, REAL SELLABLE SUPPLY, PLATFORM, MONEY FLOW, temporal history, FAILURE JOURNEY, GROUND, NATIVE, island/bottleneck/asymmetry, OPPORTUNITY, integration, and empirical final formula.

The 8 cross-layers cover destination consideration/choice, group decision, budget/WTP/price response, capacity/inventory/time, network/social diffusion, counterfactual/lost demand, intervention/causal uplift, and uncertainty decomposition.

## 8. Business-planning extension
Source routing continues through:
`KTOF Candidate → Business Readiness → Evidence Gap → Ground Research → MVP → Native Telemetry → Causal Validation → Investment Decision`.

Official evidence can establish market/regulatory structure; enterprise evidence can establish operational market behavior; Ground and Native become increasingly necessary for WTP, unit economics, capacity, actual conversion, causal lift and realized profitability.

## 9. Stop and conflict rules
Stop source expansion when the direct measurement, required coverage, methodology and necessary independent triangulation are sufficient and expected information gain is low. When credible sources conflict, preserve CONFLICTING and investigate universe, time, geography, method, definition and selection bias rather than averaging the conflict away.

## 10. KTOF relationship
KTOF consumes this evidence standard. KTOF(0) may use provisional hypotheses; KTOF(*) requires empirically estimated relationships, outcomes, uncertainty and out-of-time/out-of-segment validation. Source class never substitutes for empirical validation.
