# Korea Tourism Opportunity Intelligence Engine — Master V1

## Mission
Model the complete inbound traveler journey from origin-country awareness to post-trip behavior, mirror it against Korean supply/value chains, detect missing/broken/expensive edges, and convert validated gaps into measurable business interventions.

## Engine stack
1 Demand/Traveler: WHO WHY WHEN ORIGIN COMPETITION SEARCH BOOK_PAY MOVE STAY DO SPEND PAIN SATISFACTION.
2 Supply/Distribution: SUPPLY REAL_SELLABLE_SUPPLY PLATFORM MONEY_FLOW.
3 Time/Failure: TEMPORAL_10Y12M FAILURE_JOURNEY.
4 Reality/Behavior: GROUND_INTELLIGENCE N_TELEMETRY.
5 Diagnostics: ISLAND_BOTTLENECK_ASYMMETRY.
6 Decision: OPPORTUNITY.
7 Integration/learning: L7 + KTOF_EMPIRICAL.

## Objective hierarchy
Truth before coverage. Denominator before rate. Provenance before score. Gap before opportunity. Solvability/economics before business. Outcome before weight.

## What is complete
Source-routing and analytical architecture for all 25 axes; evidence/observation/graph structures for the public research program; final protocols for Ground, telemetry, diagnostics, opportunity, L7 and empirical calibration.

## What is not complete
Bulk historical ingestion, nationwide supplier fieldwork, live first-party telemetry, private platform/transaction data, intervention outcomes, and empirically learned KTOF weights. These are execution/data acquisition, not missing conceptual architecture.

## North-star output
An explainable Opportunity Object that answers: who wants what, when/where, what blocks them, what supply exists, why it is inaccessible, how much value is at risk, what intervention can fix it, who captures/loses value, expected economics, confidence, and evidence path.
