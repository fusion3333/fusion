## Goal
Measure what online/public data cannot: actual foreigner sellability, capacity, willingness, hidden friction, workaround, bundling and revalue potential.

## Sampling frame
Stratify by region x asset family x official/hidden supply x platform coverage x demand tier. Phase 1 Seoul/Gyeonggi 100 suppliers; deep interview top 20. Expand nationally only after pilot reliability review. Never infer national prevalence from convenience interviews.

## Supplier instrument
Identity/location/legal status; original asset/product; current price; duration; operating days/slots/max group; foreign guest share by origin if known; language actually usable; discovery channels; booking path; identity/account requirements; foreign-card/payment path; confirmation; cancellation/refund; last mile; current platform mix; realized commission/promotion burden if voluntarily disclosed; top foreign-customer pains; workarounds; willingness to receive more foreign guests; capacity to scale; bundle willingness; partner constraints; revalue ideas; evidence/confidence.

## Traveler validation instrument
Origin/WHO/WHY; discovery source; intended activity; attempted channel; failed stage/reason; workaround/substitution; actual completion; spend; pain; satisfaction; recommendation; counterfactual 'would have bought if fixed'.

## Evidence rules
Separate stated from observed. Record respondent role/date/channel. Price/capacity/payment must be timestamped. Sensitive commercial values are optional and access-controlled. Consent and minimum-necessary collection required. No hidden recording. PII is excluded from analytical graph IDs.

## Reliability
Double-code 10% of qualitative interviews; maintain codebook; resolve disagreements; evidence attachment where permitted; contradiction flag against public/platform data; revisit high-sensitivity contradictions.

## Outputs
SupplierGroundObservation, TravelerFailureObservation, SellabilityAudit, CapacityObservation, WillingnessObservation, BundleCandidate, RevalueCandidate, ContradictionCase.

Architecture is closed. Remaining work is field execution, not more generic web research.
