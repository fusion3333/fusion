# COMPETITIVE DESTINATION MIRROR — L1 EVIDENCE BATCH K
Date: 2026-08-27
Status: ACTIVE

## Objective
Measure Korea against destinations that compete for the same origin-country traveler, using the same canonical measures wherever possible.

## Priority comparator set
Japan, Taiwan, Thailand, Singapore, Vietnam, Hong Kong; expand by origin segment to China, Malaysia, Indonesia, Europe/US where relevant.

## O — Official
### Japan
JNTO publishes nationality x month visitor arrivals, purpose series and satisfaction/revisit CSV/PDF; nationality/month history is available from 2003.
https://www.jnto.go.jp/statistics/data/visitors-statistics/
https://statistics.jnto.go.jp/en/graph/

### Taiwan
Taiwan Tourism Administration database provides visitor arrivals by residence, nationality, gender, age, mode/port, length of stay, purpose and expenditure.
https://stat.taiwan.net.tw/statistics
https://stat.taiwan.net.tw/statistics/year/inbound/nationality

### Thailand
Ministry of Tourism and Sports / National Statistical Office publish foreign tourist arrivals by nationality; 2024-2025 quarterly and annual tables are available.
https://minister.mots.go.th/home

### Singapore
SingStat provides tourism statistics with resident-country and monthly cuts; use as canonical comparator for city-state accessibility, stay and spend.

### Vietnam
Viet Nam National Authority of Tourism publishes monthly international visitor totals by transport mode and source market.
https://vietnamtourism.gov.vn/en/statistic/international

### Hong Kong
HK Tourism Board/Immigration statistics can support arrivals and gateway/mode comparisons; exact dataset endpoints must be registered before use.

## E — Enterprise comparison sensors
- Air/GDS: seat capacity, fare, future booking.
- OTA: room/activity price, supply depth, cancellation terms, review volume.
- Search: Google/YouTube/social destination interest.
- Payments: cross-border spend by origin corridor.

## S — Social/VOC
For each origin country compare Korea vs alternatives on repeated themes: ease, safety, language, payment, transport, authenticity, food, nightlife, shopping, value, crowding, visa, weather and service recovery.

## R — Research
Destination competitiveness, choice-set and substitution literature; model substitution rather than assuming every Asian destination is a direct competitor.

## Canonical mirror dimensions
`ARRIVALS`, `DESTINATION_SHARE`, `AIR_ACCESS`, `AIRFARE`, `VISA`, `STAY_LENGTH`, `SPEND`, `ACTIVITY_MIX`, `SATISFACTION`, `REVISIT`, `SEARCH`, `HOTEL_PRICE`, `EXPERIENCE_SUPPLY`, `PAYMENT_ACCESS`, `MOBILITY_USABILITY`, `SEASONALITY`.

## Important rule
Same label != same statistic. Japan JNTO visitor count, Taiwan visitor arrivals and Korea KTO foreign visitor statistics must keep native definitions before harmonized ratios are derived.

## Opportunity use
Detect `KoreaUnderpenetration`: an origin segment displays high international travel propensity and strong Korea interest, but Korea's realized destination share underperforms close substitutes after controlling for access/price/visa proxies.
This remains HYPOTHESIS until panel data is built.
