# Workstream 07 — Multilingual Pain / Failure Completion Report

Date: 2026-08-28

PUBLIC_SOURCE_MAXIMUM_COMPLETE_WITH_PLATFORM_DENOMINATOR_AND_TELEMETRY_LIMITS

## What is now complete
1. National official complaint backbone is locked around KTO Tourism Complaint/Data Lab and annual complaint-analysis reports, including the officially published 2025 report.
2. 1330 is locked as the official multilingual information/interpretation/complaint route. Language/channel access is modeled as part of reporting selection rather than as neutral infrastructure.
3. Seoul foreign-tourist taxi QR complaint evidence is added as a calibration case separating 487 submitted reports in Jun-Dec 2025 from 8 violations confirmed after investigation. These are intentionally different measures.
4. O/E/S/R/G/N routing is locked for pain/failure discovery.
5. TOP15 market-language routing is fixed as a sampling/search prior, with explicit prohibition on language→nationality inference.
6. A canonical pain taxonomy spans discovery, navigation, language, identity, booking, payment, fare transparency, transport, stay, food, shopping/tax refund, experience access, accessibility, safety/scam, discrimination/service attitude, connectivity, physical burden, refund/cancellation and complaint/support.
7. Original-language preservation, translation provenance and translation confidence are mandatory.
8. Comparability rules distinguish complaint, support, review, social post, verified violation, workaround and behavioral abandonment.
9. Denominators are explicitly graded D0 NONE, D1 PLATFORM, D2 CHANNEL, D3 JOURNEY_ATTEMPT, D4 POPULATION.
10. Multilingual Pain/Failure graph is materialized with separate reporting and behavioral state machines.

## What is not claimed
- Complaint count is not population pain prevalence.
- Negative reviews are not failure events for all travelers.
- Social post volume is not incidence.
- Review language is not nationality/residence.
- A completed trip after a workaround is not friction-free.
- Absence of complaints is not zero pain.
- Before/after service changes are not automatically causal.

## Remaining non-public / non-discovery work
- Historical complaint row-level extraction and harmonization across the full 10+ year panel.
- Stable multilingual OTA/map review corpora with valid platform denominators.
- TOP15 origin-market social corpus materialization.
- Ground interviews/mystery booking for hidden offline pain.
- Owned attempt/failure/recovery telemetry for real failure-rate denominators.
- Lawful outcome linkage without invasive cross-platform identity resolution.

These are ingestion, partnership, Ground or Native telemetry problems; they do not justify keeping generic public-source discovery open.
