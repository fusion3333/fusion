# REAL SELLABLE SUPPLY — Evidence Completion — 2026-08-28

## Purpose
Distinguish supply that merely exists from supply an inbound traveler can actually discover, understand, book, pay for, reach, consume, and recover/refund when needed.

## Evidence locked
1. KTO tourism-complaint evidence documents real cases where travelers could not reserve/pay with foreign phone/card conditions; in one cited case a supplier later enabled some foreign-card payment and EN/JP/ZH notice. This proves legal/operating existence does not imply foreign-traveler transaction usability.
2. KTO global-festival management research explicitly evaluates foreigner-facing capacity, multilingual readiness, paid-program operation, product pricing/sales strategy, accommodation/food/experience bundling and access. These are direct sellability dimensions rather than mere asset existence.
3. VisitKorea/Korea Quality pages can expose traveler-facing operational attributes such as credit-card use, tax refund, foreign-language staff and reservation/product information for individual entities; these are entity-level evidence, not national coverage estimates.
4. MCST policy treats inbound convenience from entry to departure as a distinct improvement objective, reinforcing that demand/supply volume alone is insufficient.

## Sellability state machine
EXISTS → OPERATING → DISCOVERABLE → UNDERSTANDABLE → PRICE_VISIBLE → INVENTORY_VISIBLE → BOOKABLE → FOREIGNER_ELIGIBLE → PAYABLE → CONFIRMED → PHYSICALLY_ACCESSIBLE → CONSUMABLE → SUPPORTABLE/REFUNDABLE

## Measurement rule
Every state is product×channel×traveler-segment×date specific. A supplier may be sellable on one OTA and unsellable on its own site, or payable for one card/identity path and not another.

## Evidence limitation
Public evidence can define and spot-check the funnel but cannot provide a nationwide denominator for every state. Ground audit and N-telemetry are mandatory for empirical sellability rates.
