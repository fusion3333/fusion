# REAL SELLABLE SUPPLY — L1 EVIDENCE BATCH N
Date: 2026-08-27
Status: ACTIVE

## Principle
Official supply count is not equivalent to supply that a foreign traveler can actually discover, understand, book, pay for and reach.

State chain:
`REGISTERED -> OPERATING -> DISCOVERABLE -> LANGUAGE_READY -> BOOKABLE -> PAYABLE -> REACHABLE -> WILLING -> CAPACITY_READY -> RECOMBINABLE -> REAL_SELLABLE`

## O — Official
- KTO TourAPI / public tourism-content APIs: official tourism objects and regional demand APIs; use as registered/known-supply backbone, not proof of operating/bookable status.
- Local-government licensing and tourism-business registers: legal/registered state.
- KTO regional tourism-demand API combines card, mobile and navigation-derived subindicators, useful for demand context, not supplier sellability.
  https://www.data.go.kr/data/15151868/openapi.do

## E — Enterprise / platform
- Google Business Profile / Maps: discoverability, hours, website, reviews, action links where available. Google Business Profile APIs include reviews, business information, place actions and performance for authorized businesses.
  https://developers.google.com/my-business
- OTA/activity marketplaces: listing presence, price, cancellation terms, language, instant-confirm/availability where observable.
- Supplier websites: multilingual information, contact/booking/payment flows.
- Payment providers: foreign-payment acceptance.

## S — Social/VOC
Reviews and social presence indicate recent operating evidence, trust and pain, but absence of reviews does not prove closure.

## R — Research
Digital accessibility, platform adoption and tourism SME distribution research can guide state definitions but cannot substitute supplier-level verification.

## G — Ground validation
For prioritized traditional-tourism suppliers record:
`actual operating status`, `daily capacity`, `foreign-language practical ability`, `foreign customer share`, `origin countries`, `booking channel`, `foreign-card acceptance`, `international shipping`, `supplier pain`, `desire for more foreign customers`, `package/recombination willingness`, `price flexibility`, `partnership willingness`.

## N — Operational telemetry
Availability calendars, booking attempts/success, payment success, capacity utilization, cancellations/no-shows and payout are strongest evidence of sellability.

## Sellability evidence rule
No single sensor upgrades a supplier directly to REAL_SELLABLE. Each state requires evidence and timestamp. A supplier may regress (e.g. operating -> closed; payable -> payment disabled).

## Pilot
Traditional tourism in Seoul/Gyeonggi is the first ground-truth vertical. Desk research may identify 100 candidates; high-information-value subset should receive phone/site validation.
