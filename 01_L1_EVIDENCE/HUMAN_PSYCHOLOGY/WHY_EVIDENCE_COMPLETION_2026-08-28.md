# WHY + HUMAN PSYCHOLOGY — EVIDENCE COMPLETION
Date: 2026-08-28
Decision: PUBLIC_SOURCE_DISCOVERY_LOCKED / CAUSAL_CONVERSION_PARTIAL

## Scope
WHY is not one variable. It is a layered decision system:
TRIP PURPOSE → KOREA INTEREST TRIGGER → DESTINATION CONSIDERATION → KOREA CHOICE REASON → INTENT → SEARCH/PLAN/BOOK → ARRIVAL → EXPERIENCE.
Human Psychology adds latent states: image, anticipated emotion, identity fit, social proof, novelty/familiarity, perceived value, risk, effort/friction and why-now timing.

## Official backbone verified
1. KTO Foreign Tourist Survey is an approved official survey and is conducted monthly. The 2025 instrument explicitly includes trip purpose, Korea-interest trigger, Asian destinations considered, trigger for considering Asian destinations, activities/infrastructure considered before visiting Korea, information-contact/search variables, and post-trip outcomes.
2. The survey has major methodology regimes. In particular 2020-2022 were altered during COVID and must not be silently concatenated with pre-2020 series.
3. KTO Potential Foreign Tourist Survey provides a distinct pre-arrival sensor. It measures overseas travel/Korea intent and preparation/desired behavior among potential visitors. 2025 covers 26 origin markets with 16,360 general foreigners and 16,360 Korea-intenders; 2025 introduced subregional stratification and weighting of the intender sample using inbound statistics. This is a methodology break.
4. KTO country dashboards expose Korea experience/intent and, for seven origin countries, a Google-derived Korea travel interest index. The index is relative, not absolute search volume, and is intended for trend analysis.

## What public evidence can establish
Public evidence can robustly observe stated purpose, recalled triggers/reasons, consideration sets, stated intent, destination image/perception, information influences and some search-interest trends. It can compare actual visitors with potential visitors without conflating them.

## What it cannot establish by itself
Public evidence does not identify individual-level causal conversion from K-content exposure to search to booking to arrival. It cannot reliably recover unconscious motives, the counterfactual reason a non-visitor would have travelled, or desired-but-never-attempted trips. These require E/G/N sensors or experimental/quasi-experimental research.

## Methodology boundaries
- Foreign Tourist Survey: preserve annual questionnaire/sample/mode changes; COVID 2020-2022 is a hard regime flag.
- Potential Foreign Tourist Survey: 2020 pilot; 2021 expansion; 2022 panel redesign/annualization; 2025 subregional sampling and intender weighting change.
- Google Korea-travel interest: relative normalized index, only supported for Japan/Taiwan/US/Hong Kong/Philippines/Vietnam/Singapore in current public dashboard; never treat as absolute demand.

## Completion decision
Further generic web discovery has low expected information gain. The public source layer is locked. Reopen only for raw-table ingestion, newly released official waves, high-value enterprise telemetry, causal research, or Ground/N evidence.
